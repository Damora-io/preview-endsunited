Privacy

Your information stays yours.

Updated November 2026

What this site keeps

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ad trackers and no cookies on this website.

Tryout list and plant orders

  • The tryout form opens your own email app with your details filled in, addressed to endsunitedfb@gmail.com. Nothing leaves your phone or computer until you press send.
  • A plant order opens your own text or email app with the order written out. You choose who it goes to, usually the Ends United player you are buying from.
  • Your browser keeps two things on your own device: your plant order draft, so it is there if you come back, and your light or dark choice. "Start a new order" clears the draft, and clearing your browser data removes both.

Player photos and names

Player features are posted by Ends United. A parent or guardian can ask for any photo or name to come down at endsunitedfb@gmail.com.

Fonts

The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's IP address when a page opens.

Questions

Email endsunitedfb@gmail.com.
