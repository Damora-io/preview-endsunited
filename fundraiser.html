Care

Keep them warm

These are indoor plants (the tabletop greenery can go outside). Cold hurts them: never leave a plant in a car or a cold garage. Bring it inside as soon as it arrives.

Good to know

Live plants

Bi-colour is red and white plants together in one pot. Tri-colour is three colours together, usually red, white and pink. Colours can be swapped if a variety runs short. The Grinch tree is cut cedar greens in a pot, not a live tree, and its pot and bow may not match the picture.
