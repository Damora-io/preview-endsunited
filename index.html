The program

Built by pros. Developing future pros.

These aren't guest coaches. They built this organization, and they run every practice and every film session. 7v7 is a passing game, so the staff is built around the positions that define it: current pro receivers and defensive backs teaching the techniques, concepts and standards used in today's game.

The season

More than 7v7.

Everything has a purpose. Learn from current pros, compete across the border, walk onto college campuses, and get developed on and off the field.

01 · Weekly practices

Every week, under the lights.

Structured practices built for development, chemistry and competition. Receivers and DBs coached by pros who play those positions, plus quarterback development.

02 · Film room

The film room.

Film breakdown, self scout and concept study. Learn to watch the game the way the pros do, with direct feedback on your own reps.
